# Ghana filing checklist — Shopper Africa launch

**Not legal advice.** Confirm with Ghana counsel. Processes as of 2026.

Platform assumptions: Instacart-style app, Paystack + MoMo (not your own wallet), shoppers + riders, optional partner drones, no franchise territories.

## Master pack (assemble once)

Ghana Cards of directors · office lease + GhanaPost GPS · 3 name options · beneficial ownership · director phones/emails

## Phase order

1. **ORC** — incorporate company, constitution, officers, beneficial ownership, annual returns (orc.gov.gh)
2. **GRA** — TIN, tax accounts, VAT when threshold hit, PAYE when hiring
3. **Assembly** (AMA if Accra) — business operating permit
4. **DPC** — data controller registration, Act 843 (dataprotection.org.gh)
5. **RGD** — trademark (industrial property stayed at RGD; companies are at ORC)
6. **BoG** — do **not** apply for PSP if you collect via Paystack; confirm partner is on BoG approved list
7. **Paystack (or licensed PSP)** — merchant account + company bank account
8. **PCSRC** — courier/delivery licence + rider IDs (pcsrc.gov.gh)
9. **DVLA + NIC insurer** — licences, roadworthy, motor insurance
10. **FDA** — each *store* Food Hygiene Permit; platform only lists licensed vendors. Own FDA licence only if you run a dark store
11. **SSNIT + NPRA Tier 2 + GRA PAYE** — when you have employees
12. **Labour Department** — contracts; counsel memo on shopper/rider employee vs contractor
13. **NCA** — VAS / short code only if you send Ghana SMS or USSD
14. **WhatsApp / Meta** — WABA (not a Ghana statutory licence); still need DPC + opt-in
15. **GCAA** — only if *you* fly drones (ROC, registration, import). Prefer a certified partner at launch
16. **GIPA + Immigration** — only if foreign capital or expatriate staff (GIPA Act 2026, Act 1173)

## Do not take public paid orders until

- [ ] ORC company
- [ ] GRA TIN
- [ ] Assembly permit
- [ ] DPC submitted or issued
- [ ] PSP merchant live (MoMo + card)
- [ ] PCSRC started
- [ ] First stores have FDA permits on file
- [ ] Privacy + WhatsApp/SMS opt-in in the app
