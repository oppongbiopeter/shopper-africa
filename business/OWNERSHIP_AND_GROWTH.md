# Growth (platform model)

Shopper Africa is **not a franchise**. There are no territory sales, exclusive zip packages, or merchant-owned customer lists.

## Customers

All users belong to the **platform**. Merchants fulfill orders; they do not import CRM lists.

## Growth tools that remain

- Abandoned checkout capture (re-engagement)
- Referrals in local currency
- Consumer flash promos (% off, free delivery) — not franchise territory deals

## Open pool

Reassigns **jobs** (shopper / rider) when someone cannot complete work — Instacart-style labor pool, not customer transfer between stores.

## Ops coverage

Admin defines **active delivery areas** (e.g. Accra central pilot). That is operational coverage, not a product sold to franchisees.
