# Shopper Africa — Product Overview

Instacart-style grocery marketplace for Ghana and expandable across Africa.

## What it is

Mobile app (React Native) for:

- **Customers** — multi-store cart, Paystack + MoMo, tips after delivery, drone option, live tracking
- **Personal shoppers** — batch pick, chat, substitutions, multi-store
- **Riders / drones** — Okada, car, drone jobs, GPS, geofence
- **Merchants** — products, barcode, orders (platform owns customers)
- **Admins** — pricing, earnings, fleet, growth, offline sync, disputes, fulfillment toggles

## Launch market

- Primary: **Ghana** (GHS, MTN MoMo, Telecel, AirtelTigo, Paystack, VAT 15%)
- Configured for: NG, KE, ZA, CI, SN, UG, TZ, RW, EG

## Product model

- Platform-owned customers (not merchant CRM import)
- No franchise territory sales
- Offline-first outbox
- Multi-vehicle + drone eligibility with fail-over
- Configurable Instacart-style shopper earnings
